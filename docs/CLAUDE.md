## Local rules

- **Authority order A0–A7** is defined in `00_MYFI_CANONICAL_AUTHORITY.md` and
  binds every decision. A0 (actual repo state) beats every document here.
- **Never pick a document by filename label or date alone.** A newer-looking file
  is not automatically current; check the index and the authority registrations.
- `04_CURRENT_EVIDENCE/` (72 files) is append-mostly and dated. Newer real-device
  evidence beats older automated or static evidence. Read the newest file for a
  topic, not the whole directory.
- `01_CORE_AUTHORITY/` holds the frozen master plan and active overlays — the only
  place phase sequencing may be changed, and only via an approved proposal.
- **Every change gets written here, with reasoning.** Record what was checked, why
  the chosen approach seemed right, and what was rejected — not just the outcome.
- Superseded documents are listed in `00_MYFI_CANONICAL_AUTHORITY.md` §3 and
  `90_HISTORICAL_SUPERSEDED_INDEX.md`. They have no execution authority; do not
  delete them, do not act on them.
